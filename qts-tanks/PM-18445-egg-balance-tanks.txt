QTS 18445 EGG BALANCE TANKS - PM STEPS FOR APTEAN (SHORT VERSION FOR TECHS)
Tanks: 18445-101-1 and 18445-101-2 (1,200 gal, insulated, Nord agitator 3HP @ 34 RPM)
Source: QTS manual "Dejong QTS-18445-101-1 & 2" (in this folder), incl. Nord B1000 (gear unit) and B1091 (motor).
Nord's weekly noise check is in the monthly PM; the 2-yr/10,000-hr oil change is step 3 of the semi-annual.


========== MONTHLY PM ==========

EGG BALANCE TANKS (18445) - MONTHLY PM
Tank stays in service. Outside check only.
1. Agitator running: listen for noise, vibration or heat at motor/gearbox.
2. Agitator turns CLOCKWISE. No leak at top shaft seal.
3. No oil leaks at gearbox. Motor fins/fan clean.
4. Look for leaks at clamps, valves, manway and outlet.
5. Can vent and anti-siphon clear. (Blocked vent can collapse tank.)
6. Probes, cables, legs and outer shell OK. Labels in place.
7. WO for anything found.


========== SEMI-ANNUAL PM ==========

EGG BALANCE TANKS (18445) - SEMI-ANNUAL PM
LOTO agitator + pumps. Drain tank. MOVING PARTS - lock out agitator before opening manway. CONFINED SPACE - do not enter.
1. Do monthly checks.
2. Gearbox oil level: remove level plug - oil at bottom of hole. Top up with ISO VG220 mineral EP only - don't mix oils.
3. Oil change if 2 yrs / 10,000 hrs since last: drain, refill ~5.3 qt VG220, clean breather/vent screw.
4. Gearbox bolts and agitator bridge tight.
5. Open manway. Flashlight check inside: shell, agitator, spray balls clear.
6. Check bottom guide bearing and top shaft seal for wear. Replace if worn.
7. Replace tri-clamp gaskets you opened. Replace manway gasket if worn.
8. Clean can vent screen and anti-siphon cap.
9. Remove tools. Close manway. Remove LOTO.
10. Bump agitator - must turn CLOCKWISE.
Parts: gaskets EG40E3.0 / EG40E4.0 / EG40E6.0 | manway gasket MW-RND-18-GSKT | shaft seal 6"x2" Delrin + O-ring VF753A-329 | guide bearing QTS-GB-3.0-DEL | oil: ISO VG220 mineral EP (Mobilgear 600 XP 220 or equal)


========== NOT SCHEDULABLE AS MONTHLY/SEMI (NORD SERVICE) ==========

- Every 4 years / 20,000 hrs: re-lubricate gearbox bearings (NORD service; grease Petamo GHY 133N).
- Every 5 years: motor complete overhaul (NORD service).
- Every 10 years: gearbox general overhaul (NORD service).
